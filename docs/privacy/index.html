BUILT Privacy Policy

Effective July 28, 2026

BUILT is designed to work locally on your device. The developer does not operate an account system, advertising network, analytics service, or server that receives your quitting, craving, fitness, photo, or reward data.

Information stored on your device

BUILT may store information you enter or create, including your quit date, smoking pattern, identity statement, craving history, replacement actions, motivation photos, notification preferences, and reward goals. This information is stored in the app’s local container or its private App Group container so the BUILT widget can display your progress.

Apple Health

When you choose to connect Apple Health, BUILT requests permission to read workout and active-energy information so it can show training completed since quitting smoking. Health access is optional and controlled through Apple’s system permission screens and Settings. BUILT does not use Health data for advertising, tracking, or sale, and the developer does not transmit Health data to a server.

Photos

Motivation photos you add are stored locally within BUILT. The developer does not upload or receive these photos.

Notifications and widgets

Reminder preferences and widget snapshots are stored locally. Notification delivery is handled by Apple’s operating system.

Purchases

BUILT Pro is sold as a lifetime non-consumable in-app purchase through Apple. Apple processes the transaction. BUILT receives entitlement status from StoreKit so it can unlock Pro features. The developer does not receive your payment-card details.

Tracking, analytics, and advertising

BUILT does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Data deletion

Deleting BUILT from your device removes data stored in its local app container. Apple Health permissions can be changed separately in the Health app or Settings. Purchase history is maintained by Apple and may be restored through StoreKit.

Children

BUILT is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Health disclaimer

BUILT provides motivational and habit-support tools. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency care. Seek qualified medical help for health concerns or emergencies.

Changes

This policy may be updated when BUILT’s features or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.
