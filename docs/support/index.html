Quit smoking. Protect the body and life you are building.

Restore BUILT Pro

Open BUILT Settings and choose Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that originally purchased the lifetime unlock.

Apple Health is not showing data

Confirm that Health access is enabled for BUILT in the Health app or Settings. BUILT reads only the workout and active-energy data you permit.

Notifications are not appearing

Confirm that reminders are enabled inside BUILT and that iOS notification permission is enabled for BUILT in Settings.

The widget is out of date

Open BUILT once, make a small profile or reward update, and return to the Home Screen. iOS controls the exact widget refresh time.

Delete local data

Deleting BUILT from your device removes data stored in the app’s local container. Apple Health permissions and Apple purchase history are managed separately by Apple.
